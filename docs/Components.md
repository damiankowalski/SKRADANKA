# Components

The authoritative field list with defaults is generated at runtime: `{"cmd":"component.list"}`
(or the editor's Add Component menu). Definitions: `Penumbra/src/Penumbra/Scene/Components.h`.
Fields prefixed `Runtime` in C++ are read-only state, visible through `entity.get {runtime:true}` and `Entity:get()`.

| Component | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Tag, Transform | Name; local translation / rotation (Euler degrees) / scale. Always present. |
| Camera | Perspective camera; the `Primary` one renders the game. |
| Mesh, Material | `builtin:cube/sphere/plane/capsule/cylinder` or a glTF path; `HiddenNodes` lists source-file node names not to draw (unused weapons). Material overrides all mesh materials (PBR, emission, textures, translucency); `UVScale` repeats its textures across the mesh UVs (2, 2 = twice each way; the built-in cube maps every face 0..1, so set it to the face's size in metres for a texture that covers a metre) and `UVOffset` shifts where they start, in repeats. Positive scales only: a negative one mirrors the texture and turns its normal map inside out. |
| Animator | Plays the skeletal clips of a skinned/animated glTF on the same entity: `Clip`, `Speed`, `Loop`, `Playing`, `BlendDuration` (crossfade seconds when `Clip` changes); runtime: `CurrentClip`, `Time`, `Blend`, `Finished`. Driven from Lua with `anim.*`. |
| DirectionalLight, PointLight, SpotLight | Lights; `CastShadows`, `Flicker` (torches), `Enabled`. One directional light has a shadow map: the brightest enabled one with `CastShadows` (`Scene::GetShadowedDirectionalLight`); the other directional lights with `CastShadows` are left out meanwhile, in the picture and for the guards alike (unshadowed they would shine through roofs). A lightning flash takes the map from the moon while it lasts. Point and spot lights with `CastShadows` never light through walls: shadow maps (8 point, 8 spot) go to the lamps whose range reaches the view, nearest to the camera first, and one in view with no map left is left out of the picture (the F3 performance panel counts them). Without `CastShadows` a lamp shines through walls on screen while the guards still see walls block it: keep it for small lights (a range that cannot reach past a wall). |
| Environment | HDRI map, ambient, exposure, fog, SSAO, bloom, `AmbientNoise` (0-10 background noise that masks quiet sounds for NPCs). Fog (`Fog.md`; also what guards see through): `FogDensity` (per metre), `FogColor` (its own glow), height fog (`FogHeight`, `FogHeightFalloff`), volumetric light (`VolumetricFog`, `FogScattering`, `FogAnisotropy`). |
| RigidBody | Static / Dynamic / Kinematic body, mass, friction, `IsTrigger`, `ContinuousCollision`, `TransparentToSight`. |
| BoxCollider, SphereCollider, CapsuleCollider, MeshCollider | Collision shapes (an entity with colliders and no RigidBody is static). |
| CharacterController | Walking character (player, guards); driven by `character.move/jump`. |
| Script | Lua script path + per-entity property overrides. |
| AudioSource, AudioListener | 3D sound; `NoiseLoudness` (0-10) makes it audible to NPCs. |
| Surface | What a floor or object is made of: `Material` (reported as `Footstep.<Material>` / `Impact.<Material>`), `NoiseModifier` (levels added to footsteps and impacts on it), `ImpactLoudness` + `ImpactSound` for dynamic objects that clatter when they hit something. |
| NavMesh | Navmesh bake settings (cell size, agent radius/height/climb/slope); baked from static colliders at start. Runtime: `Polygons`, `BuildMilliseconds`. |
| NavAgent | Walks a CharacterController along the navmesh (`nav.move_to`): `Speed`, `TurnSpeed`, `StoppingDistance`, `FaceMovement`. Agents give way to each other: keep right when meeting head on, go round one who stands, stop beside a destination another agent stands on. Runtime: `Status`, `Destination`, `RemainingDistance`, `PartialPath`. |
| Perception | NPC eyes and ears (see `Perception.md`): vision (including `SelfShading`, how much a lit body's own bright and dark side count), suspicion tempo (`SuspicionFloor`), hearing sensitivity, reaction duration and state levels, `Diligence` against distractions, `BodyAlarm`, `ProjectileAlarm`, touch (`TouchRange`, `TouchAlarm`) and close range (`CloseRange`, `CloseRangeStimulus`); runtime: suspicion, state, reaction strength/time, stimulus kind/cause/position, alertness. |
| PerceptionTarget | Something NPCs can see: `Kind` Intruder (the player), Body (a downed NPC, found once) or Projectile (an arrow in flight, judged every frame); `Emissive` for things that glow; silhouette points, reflectance; runtime: light level, exposure. |
| Vitality | `State` Awake / Unconscious / Dead (`ai.knock_out`, the `Knockout` stim action) and `Health` (the `Damage` stim action, `ai.damage`; 0 = Dead). Downed NPCs perceive nothing and lie where they fell. |
| Ragdoll | A character whose body goes limp when knocked out or killed: its skinned model (first child with an Animator) is handed to 12 jointed bodies built from a humanoid skeleton (hips, spine, chest, head ball, arms, legs; knees and elbows are hinges), falling from its pose and speed; the entity follows the hips along the floor and its body silhouette points sit on the parts. `Enabled`, `Mass` (kg, whole body), `RadiusScale` (limb thickness), `Friction` (against floors; below the slope's tangent the body slides). Runtime: `Active`, `Awake` (false once the body has come to rest). Lua: `physics.ragdoll_start/stop/active/impulse`. |
| FogOfWar | On the player: he only sees what his own eyes see (`FogOfWar.md`). The camera's frame counts as looked at (`ConeFromCamera`: the cone starts at the camera, as wide as the picture, fading over `EdgeSoftness` just outside it; off: a cone `ViewAngle` wide around the eye where the primary camera looks), a circle of awareness around him (`AwarenessRadius`), out to `ViewDistance`, never through walls, over things lower than his eyes (`SeeOverHeight`: a surface counts as seen when the space above it is, up to the eye); the rest is drawn darkened (`Brightness`) and drained of colour (`Saturation`), and characters there are not drawn (`HideActors`, kept for `MemoryTime` after they leave the view). The eye sits `EyeHeight` above the origin, lower when a CharacterController crouches, following the crouch at `EyeSpeed` per second. `Lean` moves it sideways, square to the view (metres, + right; the player's Q/E). Runtime: `Active`, `Eye`, `Forward`, `EyeHeightNow`, `HiddenActors`. Lua: `fog.*`. |
| Flame | Procedural animated fire (height, radius, colour, intensity, speed); doused/ignited together with lights. |
| FogVolume | A box of fog (`HalfExtents` in metres, rotates with the entity, scale ignored), taking effect over `EdgeFade` from its sides. `Mode` `Add`: extra fog, `Density` per metre; `Clear`: an interior the level's fog stays out of. Lit like the rest of the fog and seen through the same way by guards (`Fog.md`). |
| SmokeVolume | Sphere of smoke that hides silhouettes (and renders as smoke); optional lifetime. |
| StimSource, StimResponse | Act/React: emit named stims (Fire, Water, ...) by contact / radius / continuously; react with rules (extinguish, ignite, explode, spawn, impulse, sound, noise, script event, knockout, damage, alarm: an NPC hit searches where the stim came from). |
| Prefab | Records which prefab an entity tree came from. |
