# The stress test: the first mission at the finished map's density

Status: **A8 of `MissionOne.md`, 2026-11-27, waiting for the owner's approval** (built ahead of its brief on the
night the owner left the decisions to the agent; the brief is at the end). It measures what the engine packages for
the big map (A1-A7) must bring down, and stays as the scene they are measured on, before and after.

## What it is

`Scenes/Stress.pscene`, generated by `build_stress()` in `Tools/BuildDemoProject.py`: the first mission's blockout
(`MissionOneBlockout.md`) with what the finished map is expected to hold (`MissionOne.md`, "The mission as the owner
described it") added as plain boxes, cylinders and spheres, each its own entity, as props are today:

| | Blockout | Stress test | Expected in the finished map |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture | about 480 boxes (whole walls) | + 2,056 pieces: skirtings and cornices on every wall, pilasters, window frames and cornices on the houses, posts along the vines | 1,500-2,500 pieces |
| Props | about 60 (furniture, loot) | + 2,500 in 22 areas: rooms, the streets' edges, the square, the churchyard, the vineyard, the kitchen yard; 35% on shelves | 1,500-3,000 from 120-160 models |
| Lights | 44 lamps and 3 lanterns | + 135 candles: 179 lamps and 3 lanterns, all casting shadows | 150-250 |
| Meshes in all | 590 | 5,281 | |
| People | 11 guards, 2 servants | the same | 8-14 guards, 3-6 servants, 6-10 townsfolk |

The added pieces have no colliders (small clutter has none in the finished map either), so the navmesh, the patrols
and the playtester's route stay the blockout's: the same route runs on both scenes.

## How it is measured

`python Tools/Playtest/benchmark.py --scene Scenes/Stress.pscene --route Playtest/Mission1/BackLanes --notarget`
(`docs/Playtest.md`, "Benchmark"): the playtester's bot walks the mission's whole route (about 9.5 minutes of game)
in a 1600 x 900 window without vsync, while the runtime logs its frame statistics twice a second (`--perf-out`).
`--notarget` keeps the guards from catching the thief in the stress test's extra candlelight, so both scenes are
measured over the whole route.

**Not measured yet** (the night's work stopped here). The blockout's own run, for comparison: frame 12.0 ms median,
13.1 ms at 95%, GPU 11.8 ms, about 2,400 draw calls, up to 34 lamps in view left without a shadow map; a 150 ms
hitch at every piece of loot taken (the whole navmesh was rebuilt: fixed here, objects narrower than an agent no
longer rebuild it).
